## 🎶 About

**Mehfil-e-Ghazal** is a web-based music experience created to bring the feeling of a traditional Ghazal mehfil into the digital world.

Instead of a conventional music-player interface, the website combines:

- 🎵 Ghazal music
- 🪔 Warm ambient lighting
- 📻 Vintage gramophone aesthetics
- 🎧 Custom music controls
- 👏 Live **Wah!** reactions
- 🟢 Live audience presence
- 📱 Mobile support
- 🖥️ Desktop support
- 🎛️ OS-level media controls
- 🌅 Three immersive atmosphere modes

> **Sit back. Listen. Feel the Ghazal.**

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎵 Custom Music Player

- Play / Pause
- Next / Previous
- Smooth progress bar
- Current playback time
- Total duration
- Song seeking
- Volume control
- Album artwork
- Song title and artist

### 🎧 Media Session API

The player integrates with the browser's **Media Session API** to provide supported system-level media controls.

Users can control the website's music from compatible:

- Lock screens
- Notification panels
- Bluetooth/headset controls
- Browser media controls

The current song metadata includes:

- Title
- Artist
- Album artwork

Supported actions include:

- Play
- Pause
- Next
- Previous
- Seek

### 🌅 Three Atmosphere Modes

Choose the visual mood of your mehfil:

#### ☀️ Day Mode
A brighter and softer atmosphere designed for daytime listening.

#### 🌆 Evening Mode
A warm golden atmosphere inspired by a traditional evening mehfil.

#### 🌙 Night Mode
A deeper, darker and more intimate atmosphere designed for late-night Ghazal sessions.

The atmosphere changes the **lighting, ambience, and visual mood** while keeping the core music-player experience consistent.

> **Choose your mood. Enter your mehfil.**

### 🪔 Immersive Mehfil Environment

The visual experience is inspired by a traditional Indian **baithak** featuring:

- Vintage gramophone
- Warm glowing lamps
- Traditional musical atmosphere
- Wooden textures
- Soft ambient lighting
- Cinematic background

### 👏 Live "Wah!" Reaction

Listeners can react to the current Ghazal using the **Wah!** button.

The reaction includes:

- Live count
- Animated reaction
- Shared audience interaction

### 🟢 Live Audience

A live audience indicator shows how many listeners are currently connected to the mehfil.

### 🔊 Volume Control

- Custom volume control
- Vertical slider on mobile
- Live volume percentage
- Expand/collapse interaction
- Outside-click closing
- Smooth volume adjustment
